# User Guides for Merch Pre-Orders

These guides reflect the current merch pre-order flow in the NELPAC system.

## Guide 1: How to Place a Pre-Order

1. Sign in to the NELPAC system and open the Forms Center from the user dashboard.
2. Choose the Merch Pre-Order option and select an open merch form that your church wants to order from.
3. Review the merch details, including the item image, description, price, and any instructions shown on the form.
4. Fill in your church information, including district, local church, local church president, and president contact number.
5. Enter the quantity you want to order. For shirts, select the available colors and enter quantities for each size. For other merch types, enter the total number of items.
6. Complete any additional custom fields required by the form and provide your GCash payment details, including sender name, payment date, reference number, and proof of payment image.
7. Review everything carefully, then submit the pre-order. After submission, wait for the admin review and confirmation.

## Guide 2: How to Buy Pre-Order Merchandise

1. Go to the Forms Center and open the merch pre-order section from the user portal.
2. Choose the specific merch item you want to purchase and read the design, price, and payment instructions.
3. Make sure your profile is complete and that your local church is already registered in the system before you continue.
4. Enter the number of items you want to buy. If the item is a shirt, break the order down by color and size; if it is another merch type, enter the total quantity.
5. Fill in the required church contact information and any custom form sections that the organizer asks for.
6. Pay through GCash using the listed account details, then upload a clear photo of the payment proof with the date, time, and reference number visible.
7. Submit the order and keep the confirmation page or receipt for your records. Your order will be sent to the admin for verification.
